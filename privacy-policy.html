Privacy Policy — Bug Report Recorder

Last updated: September 2026

Bug Report Recorder is a browser extension that helps developers capture screen recordings, console errors, and failed network requests to create shareable bug reports. This policy explains exactly what the extension captures, where that data goes, and what control you have over it.

In short: everything the extension captures stays on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to us, or to any third party, unless you personally choose to share an exported file with someone.

What the extension captures

While you are actively recording, the extension captures:

  • Screen recording of the browser tab you choose to record, including any audio playing in that tab.
  • Console errors and warnings logged by the page.
  • Uncaught JavaScript exceptions and unhandled promise rejections.
  • Failed network requests (via fetch and XMLHttpRequest), including the request URL, query parameters, request payload, and response body, for requests that fail or return an error status.

The extension only captures this information while you have actively started a recording. It does not run in the background, and does not monitor your browsing when a recording is not in progress.

Automatic redaction of sensitive data

Before any request payload, query parameter, or response body is stored, the extension automatically scans for common sensitive field names — such as password, token, apiKey, authorization, cookie, and similar — and replaces their values with [REDACTED]. This reduces, but does not guarantee elimination of, sensitive data appearing in a report; see "Your responsibility when sharing reports" below.

Where your data is stored

  • Captured logs are held temporarily in the extension's local browser storage while a recording is in progress.
  • When you stop recording, the video, logs, and a viewer page are bundled into a single .zip file and downloaded directly to your own device, using your browser's normal download mechanism.
  • We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to your recordings, logs, or any captured data. Nothing described above is transmitted anywhere by the extension.

Data related to your license

If you purchase Bug Report Recorder, license validation is handled by our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy (acting as merchant of record). The extension sends your license key to Lemon Squeezy's License API solely to confirm it is valid and to manage device activations. This is the only data the extension transmits over the network on its own initiative. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for how they handle payment and license data.

Your responsibility when sharing reports

Exported bug reports may still contain sensitive information that our automatic redaction does not catch — for example, personal data visible on screen during the recording, or content in an unusual field name we don't recognize. Please review an exported report before sharing it with others, particularly outside your own team or organization.

Permissions the extension requests

  • activeTab / tabCapture — required to record the tab you choose.
  • scripting — required to inject the scripts that capture console errors and network requests.
  • storage — required to temporarily hold recording state and your license activation locally.
  • offscreen — required by Chrome's architecture to perform the actual recording.
  • host permissions (all sites) — required so the extension can capture errors on whichever site you choose to record; it does not act on any site unless you start a recording.

Children's privacy

Bug Report Recorder is a developer tool not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
